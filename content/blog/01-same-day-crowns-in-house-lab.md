A tooth usually breaks at the worst moment: biting into something hard at dinner, or a week before a wedding. Most people expect what they've been told before. A temporary cap, a week or two of waiting, and a second visit for the real one.

For many of our patients in Rajouri Garden, that wait doesn't happen any more. The reason is a room down the corridor from the treatment chairs.

## A lab on the same floor

Our dental lab, Titanium Arts Dental Studio, is inside the clinic. It has its own milling machines and furnaces, and the technicians work a few steps away from where you're sitting. So a crown doesn't need to travel to an outside lab and back.

## What happens on the day

First the tooth is prepared and scanned with an intraoral scanner. There's no tray of putty to bite into, just a small camera moved around your teeth for a minute or two.

The scan goes straight to the lab, where the crown is designed on screen to fit your bite and match the teeth next to it. It's then milled from a single block of E-max, a glass-ceramic that looks very like natural enamel, and fired in the furnace.

While that's happening you can rest in the lounge, step out for a coffee, or answer a few emails. When the crown is ready, I try it in, check the bite and the colour, and bond it in place. You leave with the final crown, not a temporary.

## When one visit isn't enough

Same-day crowns don't suit every tooth. If the tooth has an active infection, it needs a root canal first, and sometimes that takes more than one sitting. Zirconia crowns and bridges of several teeth usually take a second visit, because zirconia needs a long firing in the furnace. I'll tell you at the check-up which one your tooth needs, and why.

## Questions

**Can I get a crown in one day?**
Often, yes. At Dr Sobti's Dental Care in Rajouri Garden, E-max crowns are designed and milled in our own lab and fitted the same day, when the tooth is healthy enough.

**Is a same-day crown a temporary crown?**
No. It's the final crown, milled from a block of E-max glass-ceramic and bonded in place before you leave.

**Which crowns need a second visit?**
Zirconia crowns, bridges of several teeth and teeth that need treatment for infection first usually take more than one visit.
